Welcome to Fizz

A contemporary stay in the centre of Mataram.

Fizz Hotel Lombok sits on Jl. Majapahit in Mataram's business district, a short drive from the city's main shopping, museums and government offices. The hotel pairs practical, well-kept rooms with an artistic interior: murals and art installations run through the corridors, and each room carries its own mural.

A city hotel with its own character.

Fizz Hotel Lombok opened in 2015 and was renovated in 2018. Across four floors, its 43 air-conditioned rooms are designed for travellers who want a comfortable, well-located base in Mataram without fuss.

What sets the hotel apart is its art. Murals and installations appear throughout the building, and every guest room has a mural of its own.
